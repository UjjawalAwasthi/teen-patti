Teen Patti

One table, up to 60 players, 5-deck shoe

Hand rankings
  1. Trail: three of a kind (A-A-A highest)
  2. Pure Sequence: three in a row, same suit
  3. Sequence: three in a row (A-K-Q highest, A-2-3 lowest)
  4. Colour: three of the same suit
  5. Pair: two of the same rank
  6. High Card: none of the above

Ties: higher cards win, then suits (♠ > ♥ > ♦ > ♣). If hands are identical, whoever asked for the Show loses.
